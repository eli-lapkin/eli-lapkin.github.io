Privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026

This covers the Nibblr waitlist on joinnibblr.com. The Nibblr app will have its own privacy policy when it launches.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • If you choose to answer: the East Village spots you already go to.
  • Which link brought you to the waitlist, such as a friend's share link or a flyer.
  • A random code we create when you join. It's in your share link, so when friends join through it we know you sent them.

How we use it

  • To email you about Nibblr's launch in the East Village: which spots are joining, and when the app is ready.
  • To choose which restaurants to invite first. We may tell a restaurant how many people on our list named it, but never who.
  • To see how people hear about Nibblr, and who brought friends along.

We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Sign-ups are collected by Formspark, our form provider, and kept in our Google Workspace. When we send emails, we may use an email service to do it. These providers handle your information only on our behalf.

In your browser

When you join, your code is saved in your browser so the page can show your share link when you come back. It isn't used for tracking or ads. Clearing your browser's data for joinnibblr.com removes it.

Visits

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits to this site and see how fast it loads. It doesn't use cookies or follow you across other sites.

Leaving the list

Every email we send has a way to unsubscribe. To have your information deleted, email hello@joinnibblr.com and we'll remove it.

Questions

Email hello@joinnibblr.com.
