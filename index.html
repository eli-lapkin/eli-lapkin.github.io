How it works

Simple for diners. Powerful for restaurants.

🍜

Eat at local spots

Grab lunch, pick up a coffee, treat yourself to dessert at any Nibblr restaurant.

📲

Pay, then tap

After you pay, tap your phone on the Nibblr tag at the register. One punch per visit, and they add up across the whole network.

🎁

Spend punches anywhere

Trade 3, 5, 10 or 15 punches for free items and discounts at any Nibblr spot, not just the one where you earned them.

For restaurants

Partner with us to build something your neighborhood needs.

🎨

Your rewards, your menu

Choose what you offer at each punch level, like a free coffee, a side, or money off a meal. Every spot prices rewards in the same punches.

🏷️

Nothing to install

A small tag at the register is all it takes. No new hardware, no POS integration, nothing for your staff to learn beyond "pay, then tap."

📊

Know your regulars

See punches, redemptions, and the new diners who found you through other Nibblr spots.

📣

Grow with the network

Diners collecting punches nearby discover you, and early partners help shape the network as it expands across the city.
